# Categories and Definitions
- Customer Data Scope & Carve-outs
  - Exclusions or limitations in vendor policies that create gaps in protection for customer-uploaded data. Vendor's standard terms may not apply to the most sensitive customer content, creating uncertainty about how proprietary, confidential, or regulated data will be protected.

- Data Use & Purpose Limitation
  - How vendors use customer data beyond core service delivery, including for marketing, analytics, personalization, telemetry, or other secondary purposes. Concerns about inadequate purpose limitation and potential data monetization.

- Privacy & Regulatory Compliance
  - Compliance with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, Reg S-P), Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), and handling of sensitive regulated data (MNPI). Includes privacy commitments, regulatory alignment, and privacy rights programs.

- Contract Modification & Governance
  - Vendor's ability to unilaterally modify terms without customer consent, change management processes, notification requirements, and governance transparency. Includes vendor control over terms updates and supplemental agreements.

- Cross-Border Data Transfers
  - Issues related to international data transfers, data residency requirements, jurisdictional concerns, governing law, and applicable venue. Includes transfer mechanism adequacy (SCCs, DPF) and jurisdictional exposure.

- Third-Party Sharing & Sub-processors
  - Vendor's sharing of data with sub-processors, affiliates, partners, or other third parties, and the complexity this adds to oversight and accountability. Includes concerns about sub-processor transparency and customer objection rights.

- Data Security & Encryption
  - Security controls including encryption, multi-factor authentication, access controls, and safeguards for protecting customer data. Covers both the presence of strong security measures and their absence.

- Data Lifecycle & Portability
  - Data retention periods, deletion procedures, lifecycle management, post-termination handling, data portability, export capabilities, and migration support. Covers the full data lifecycle from retention to deletion and exit.

- Data Ownership & Control
  - Issues related to intellectual property rights, licensing terms, and control over customer data. Includes vendor claims of perpetual or irrevocable rights to customer content, and lack of clarity about data ownership and customer control rights.

- Operational Burden & Responsibility Allocation
  - Assignment of security, configuration, or access management responsibilities to the customer rather than the vendor. Concerns about vendors shifting critical operational burdens onto the customer organization.

- Liability & Legal
  - Limitations of vendor liability, warranty disclaimers, indemnification terms, liability caps, available remedies, and dispute resolution processes (arbitration, forum selection, venue restrictions).

- Audit, Transparency & Incidents
  - Audit rights, security certifications (SOC, ISO), transparency reports, third-party attestations, breach notification procedures, and incident response timelines. Covers oversight mechanisms and incident handling.
