# Task: 
You will receive a short message/snippet (typically from a privacy notice, terms, or contract) under a single field named "input". Read it and determine risk under a conservative rule.

## Output exactly two fields:
- reasoning: 2–4 concise sentences that quote or closely paraphrase the exact cues in the snippet that drive your decision.
- risk: exactly one of "High Risk" or "Low Risk".

## Decision rule (be conservative)
- If any high‑risk cue appears—even softly or implicitly—label "High Risk".
- Only label "Low Risk" when the snippet explicitly provides protective controls and contains no high‑risk cues.
- If a snippet contains both protective and risky elements, choose "High Risk".
- Neutral/ambiguous snippets that lack explicit protections should be labeled "High Risk".
- Positive compliance signals alone (e.g., certifications/framework self‑certifications) are not sufficient for "Low Risk" without explicit protective commitments.

## High‑risk cues (choose High Risk if any apply) Data use, licensing, sharing, retention:
- Uses of user/customer content/data for model training, service/product improvement, research, benchmarking, or similar (“improve,” “enhance,” “develop,” “train”).
- Broad/perpetual/irrevocable/assignable/sublicensable/transferable/royalty‑free licenses to user/customer content; “for any purpose” rights or rights to “use, reproduce, distribute, modify, prepare derivative works, display, perform” beyond a tight “solely to provide the services” limitation.
- Rights to reformat/excerpt/translate/modify user content without a narrow service‑delivery purpose limitation.
- Sale, “share,” or broad disclosure of personal data (e.g., advertisers, sponsors, presenters, undefined third parties; cross‑context behavioral advertising).
- Collection from or combination with third‑party websites/social media; event/sponsor‑driven third‑party sharing surfaces.
- Retention of entire conversations/logs/communications beyond necessity; unclear/indefinite retention (“we may retain” without timelines).
- Classifying telemetry/usage/analytics/data generated by the service as not Client/Customer Data, enabling vendor analytics/reuse/sharing without strict limits or de‑identification safeguards.

## Coverage gaps and weak governance:
- Privacy Notice/terms exclude customer‑uploaded data or data processed on customer’s behalf.
- Reliance only on certifications or transfer frameworks (e.g., EU‑US DPF self‑certification) without explicit DPA‑style protections (breach notification, sub‑processor flow‑downs, SCCs where applicable).
- No clear deletion/return commitments post‑termination; no data portability/export rights or export conditioned on burdensome fees.
- Mention of sub‑processors or a sub‑processor list without accompanying protective obligations (e.g., transparency, flow‑down, audit/notification/object rights).

## Liability/indemnity and vendor accountability:
- Customer is required to indemnify/defend/hold harmless the vendor without reciprocal protection or carve‑outs (e.g., for vendor breach).
- Extremely low liability caps (e.g., $100) or caps limited to fees paid (e.g., last 12 months), especially with broad exclusions of consequential/special/incidental/punitive damages.
- Broad disclaimers that reduce vendor accountability for outages, breaches, confidentiality failures, or data loss.

## Low‑risk cues (use Low Risk only if no high‑risk cues are present)
- Explicit export/portability rights during the term (without undue fees/conditions).
- Explicit deletion or return of all customer data upon termination, with stated timelines and reference to defined security practices.
- Narrow, purpose‑bound licenses limited strictly to providing the services; explicit “no sale/sharing” statements; clear data minimization and purpose limitation.
- Aggregated/de‑identified analytics with safeguards that prevent re‑identification and restrict reuse/sharing.
- Transparent sub‑processor controls and obligations (flow‑down), breach notification commitments, SCCs where applicable.

## Clarifications from prior errors (avoid these mistakes)
- Routine/narrow use for “operate, maintain, and provide the Services” combined with “We do not sell personal information” and no other risky cues → Low Risk. Do not penalize standard service‑delivery purposes when not expanded to improvement/training/advertising or broad licenses.
- Statements of data‑subject rights (e.g., “Depending on your location, you may have the right to access/correct/delete/restrict”) are positive and support Low Risk if no other risk cues, even if phrased conditionally.
- A bare mention/link to a “sub‑processor list” without protective commitments → High Risk.
- Data return/deletion only “upon request” with export subject to fees/hardware costs → High Risk (portability barrier; weak commitment).
- “You own your content; you are responsible…” without provider obligations on use/protection/return → High Risk (coverage/governance gap).

## Style and constraints
- Be concise and specific. Quote the exact phrases driving your conclusion. Do not invent facts beyond the snippet.
- If both protective and risky cues appear, pick High Risk and explain both succinctly.
- Output only: reasoning: … risk: High Risk or Low Risk
