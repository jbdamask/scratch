Speed Reader is a single-page web application that helps you read text faster using the RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) technique.

## How It Works

Instead of moving your eyes across lines of text, RSVP displays one word at a time at a fixed focal point. Each word is positioned so that its Optimal Recognition Point (ORP) - highlighted in red - stays centered on a guide line. This eliminates eye movement and allows for faster reading speeds.

## Features

- **File upload** - Load any .txt, .md, or .text file
- **Adjustable speed** - Set your reading pace from 100 to 1000 words per minute
- **ORP highlighting** - The optimal recognition point is highlighted in red and always centered
- **Progress tracking** - See your progress through the text with a progress bar
- **Keyboard shortcuts**:
  - `Space` - Play/Pause
  - `Arrow Up/Down` - Adjust speed
  - `R` - Reset to beginning

## Usage

1. Click "Choose Text File" to upload a text file
2. Adjust the speed slider to your preferred WPM
3. Press Play or hit Space to start reading
4. Use Pause or Space to stop at any time
